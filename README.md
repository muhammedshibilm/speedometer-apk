# 🏎️ Speedy Tutorial

Welcome to **Speedy**, your premium companion for every journey. This tutorial will walk you through the key features and tips to make the most out of your speedometer application.

---

## 🚦 Getting Started

When you first open Speedy, you'll land on the **Drive Page**. To begin tracking:
1. Tap the **"START TRIP"** button at the bottom.
2. Grant Location Permissions if prompted.
3. Watch as the futuristic gauge updates with your real-time speed.

---

## 🛰️ Precision Tracking

Speedy uses advanced **GPS filtering** (including Kalman filters) to ensure:
- **Low-speed stability**: No more "jumping" speed while standing still.
- **High accuracy**: Reliable readings even on winding roads.
- **Signal Status**: Check the signal bars at the top left to ensure you have a strong GPS lock.

---

## 🌓 Themes & HUD Mode

### HUD Mode (Heads-Up Display)
Driving at night? Tap the **HUD MODE** block. The display will flip horizontally. Place your phone on your dashboard to see the speed reflected clearly on your windshield.

### Light & Dark Themes
Switch between sleek **Dark Mode** (perfect for night driving) and vibrant **Light Mode** (best for daytime) using the toggle at the top right, or let it follow your system settings.

---

## 🔔 Smart Alerts

Stay safe and avoid tickets by setting a speed limit:
1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Adjust the **Speed Limit** slider.
3. Choose your **Alert Mode**:
   - **Vibration Only**: Feel a subtle pulse on your wrist or in your pocket.
   - **Sound Only**: Hear a system notification chime.
   - **Both**: Maximum awareness.
   - **None**: For a focused, whisper-quiet drive.

---

## 📂 Managing Your Trips

Once you finish a trip:
1. Tap **"END TRIP"**.
2. Give your journey a **Name** (e.g., "Commute home").
3. View your history in the **Trips** tab.
   - **Max Speed**: See how fast you went.
   - **Average Speed**: Know your efficiency.
   - **Distance & Duration**: Track your travel stats.

---

## 💡 Pro Tips

- **Windshield Reflection**: Use HUD mode at night for a high-tech, distracted-free experience.
- **Battery Life**: Speedy uses GPS actively. For long trips, we recommend keeping your phone plugged into a charger.
- **Signal Strength**: If the speed is stuck or inaccurate, ensure you have a clear view of the sky.

---
